NEON CITY RUSH

An original top-down crime sandbox. Steal cars, grab cash, outrun the cops.

W A S D / ArrowsDrive or walk
EEnter / exit / carjack a car
SpaceHandbrake (car) or shoot (on foot)
Mouse / FAim with the mouse, click or hold F to shoot (on foot or in a car)
ShiftSprint (on foot)
RReset the game
EscPause

Collect the green $ pickups. Hurting people or stealing cars raises your wanted level; stay out of sight to cool off, or drive into a purple HIDE circle to lose the cops. Your car is slow at first: drive to the orange GARAGE to spend cash on upgrades, and the red GUN SHOP to shoot faster. Take delivery jobs for big cash: drive to A, then B. Desktop keyboard required.
